Sam is a Product Manager & Maker in Scotland

Welcome to my personal website. Version… honestly, I've lost count.

By day, I'm a product manager. By night, I'm a novice web designer, compulsive tinkerer and newsletter writer.

This site is where the tinkering ends up. I've been rebuilding it for over a decade: part self-expression, part test bed for whatever new tech has caught my eye.

This time I ditched Webflow and rebuilt the whole thing from scratch with AI: four days, mostly Claude Opus 5.5, more than a billion tokens and over 20,000 lines of code.

The result is a bento box of my favourite Apple apps, shrunk down into widgets.

If you own one of the billion-plus Apple devices out there, this should feel familiar. If not, poke around anyway. Everything’s clickable and nothing’s breakable. Probably.
